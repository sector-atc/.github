# Community conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable. Critique ideas and code rather than people.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content and restrict participation. Report concerns privately through the maintainer contact link on https://github.com/Tfc538; do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers will consider reports in context and protect reporter privacy where possible.
